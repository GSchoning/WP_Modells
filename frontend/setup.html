What you're looking at

Active cells are the modelled domain — the formation extent from the properties CSV.

Outcrop / recharge cells receive recharge from above and carry rejected-recharge drains at the minimum in-cell DEM elevation. Non-outcrop cells get zero recharge.

No-flow boundary covers every natural pinch-out edge — the audit showed the perimeter is almost entirely a thin fringe (median 6–19 m thick). Steady-state discharge happens through the outcrop drains.

Far-field boundary cells are the western/southern truncation faces where the Precipice continues beyond the model frame — GHBs per the UWIR 2019 design (Fig A1-14). CHD cells appear here only if a legacy mode or convergence fallback is active.
