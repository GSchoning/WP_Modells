What you're looking at

Active cells are the modelled domain — the formation extent from the properties CSV.

Outcrop / recharge cells define the recharge zone; the OGIA steady-state recharge field applies to the exposed northern belt.

Drains simulate rejected recharge at the minimum in-cell DEM elevation. Bright cells are flowing at steady state (linearised into GHBs for the transient runs so superposition holds); faint cells are dry.

GHB far-field cells are the western truncation strip where the Precipice continues beyond the model frame — per UWIR 2025 (Fig F.1-15), with heads from the calibrated pilot values (Fig G.1-39).

No-flow boundary covers every natural pinch-out edge — the boundary audit showed the perimeter is almost entirely a thin fringe (median 6–19 m thick).

CHD cells appear only in legacy modes or if the configured boundary failed to converge and the bootstrap fell back — normally this layer is empty.
